## Scoring Rubric (0-100 scale)

**Token Efficiency (40 points)**:
- 0-2000 tokens total: 40 points (excellent)
- 2001-3500 tokens: 30 points (good)
- 3501-5000 tokens: 20 points (acceptable)
- 5001-7500 tokens: 10 points (needs splitting)
- 7500+ tokens: 0 points (critical - must split)

**Tier Structure (30 points)**:
- Metadata tier (frontmatter): Well-structured (10 pts)
- Orchestrator tier (main skill.md): Clear, concise instructions (10 pts)
- Resource tier (references/): Proper lazy loading of details (10 pts)

**Lazy Loading (20 points)**:
- Resources properly separated: 10 points
- No inline large code blocks in orchestrator: 5 points
- References linked but not inlined: 5 points

**Metadata Quality (10 points)**:
- Complete frontmatter (name, description, triggers): 5 points
- Proper tool permissions (not overly broad): 5 points
